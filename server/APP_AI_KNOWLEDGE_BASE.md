# DIU Ride - Daffodil International University Campus Peer Commute Guide & Knowledge Base

## 1. System Overview & Vision
DIU Ride is an exclusive, safe, non-commercial peer-to-peer campus ride-sharing and carpooling platform built specifically for students, faculty, and administrative staff of Daffodil International University (Ashulia Permanent Campus & Transit Points across Dhaka).

The primary purpose is mutual varsity community support:
- Helping students commute affordably and safely to and from classes.
- Allowing motorbike owners (student bikers) to share their empty pillion seat and split actual fuel expenses.
- Minimizing traffic congestion around Ashulia Highway and Mirpur/Uttara corridors.
- Zero commercial commissions — it is purely student-to-student community cooperation.

---

## 2. Dual Commute Roles & Switching
The application offers two streamlined modes accessible at any time from the top mode toggle bar:
1. **Passenger Mode**:
   - For students looking for a safe, convenient ride to campus or returning home.
   - Allows instant search by destination (e.g. "Mirpur 10", "Academic Building 4 / AB4", "Uttara", "DSC Campus").
   - Instant booking on scheduled biker trips.
   - "Schedule / Throw Request" feature to post custom pickup, dropoff, proposed fare, and departure time if no matching bike is found immediately.
2. **Biker / Rider Mode**:
   - For students with registered motorcycles who ride to campus.
   - Dedicated "Rider Section" focused purely on offering rides, managing published rides, tracking passenger requests, and monitoring trip confirmations.
   - Keeps the biker's workflow clutter-free and straightforward.

## 4. How to Use the App (Step-by-Step User Tutorial)

### For Passenger Students:
1. **Find a Ride**:
   - Check the "Departing Soon" carousel or search your target location in the search bar.
   - Each card displays biker name, varsity department, motorbike model, departure time, available seats, and fuel contribution.
2. **Book a Seat**:
   - Tap "Book Seat" on the card.
   - Confirm your pickup and drop-off points.
   - Once confirmed, you can chat directly with your biker.
3. **Throw / Post a Ride Request**:
   - If no biker is listed for your exact desired time, tap the "Schedule / Throw request" button.
   - Enter your pickup, dropoff, desired departure time, and proposed fare (typically 30-80 BDT).
   - Bikers traveling that way can view and accept your request.
4. **Live GPS Tracking & ETA**:
   - When the trip begins, open the tracking screen to view the live GPS movement, estimated arrival time, and start/end coordinates.
5. **Private Chat**:
   - Dedicated 1-on-1 private messaging to communicate meetup landmarks. Messages are private only between the confirmed passenger and rider.

### For Biker / Rider Students:
1. **Publish a Trip**:
   - Switch to Biker Mode and tap "Offer Ride".
   - Select departure location, destination, departure time, vehicle details, and seat fare.
2. **Manage Passenger Requests**:
   - Inspect the "Student Passenger Requests" section to see classmates looking for rides in your direction.
   - Accept requests that align with your schedule.
3. **Trip Lifetime & Auto-Cleanup Rules**:
   - If a ride departure time passes by more than 10 minutes without any reserved passengers, the post auto-expires and is cleaned up.
   - Completed rides are archived into your commute history.
   - If a trip is cancelled in time, the post refreshes back to available status.

## 5. Peer Fuel Contribution Guidelines
- DIU Ride is strictly peer fuel-sharing (non-commercial).
- Recommended fuel contribution rates based on distance:
  - Short distances (DSC to Baipail / Birulia): 20 - 40 BDT.
  - Medium distances (DSC to Mirpur 10 / Gabtoli / Savar): 40 - 65 BDT.
  - Longer distances (DSC to Uttara / ECB Chattar / Dhanmondi): 60 - 90 BDT.
- Payment is settled directly and transparently between peers (Cash / bKash / Nagad).

## 6. Safety, Security & Varsity Code of Conduct
1. **Helmet Requirement**: Safety is paramount. Bikers should carry a clean spare helmet or notify the passenger in advance.
2. **Emergency SOS Broadcast**:
   - In any safety concern or accident, tap the Red SOS button on the ride screen.
   - Instantly broadcasts GPS coordinates and alert details to the DIU Varsity Security team and emergency contacts.
3. **Varsity Verification**:
   - Platform is restricted to DIU community members with verified `@diu.edu.bd` university accounts and student IDs.
4. **Discipline & Mutual Respect**:
   - Always be punctual at agreed meetup spots.
   - Treat campus peers with cordiality, brotherhood, and respect.
